Computer science is the study of computation, algorithms, and data structures, forming the foundation of modern technology. It encompasses various fields, including artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, software development, and data science. Computer scientists design and optimize algorithms to solve complex problems efficiently, enabling advancements in machine learning, cloud computing, and robotics. With the rapid growth of technology, computer science continues to shape industries such as healthcare, finance, and entertainment, driving innovation and improving everyday life. As the digital world expands, the demand for skilled computer scientists remains high, making it a dynamic and ever-evolving field
